Privacy Policy

Phone Cleaner

Developer: StructLabs Package ID: in.structlabs.phonecleaner Effective Date: November 25, 2026
100% On-Device Media Privacy: Your photos, videos, thumbnails, and Private Vault files are processed and stored strictly on your device. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with our servers.

1. Overview

StructLabs ("we", "our", or "us") built the Phone Cleaner Android application (in.structlabs.phonecleaner) as an ad-supported storage and gallery organization utility. This Privacy Policy explains what permissions the app requests, how your media and preferences are handled, and what data third-party advertising services may collect.

2. On-Device Photo & Video Access

Phone Cleaner requests read access to your device gallery (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older Android versions) strictly to:

  • Group your photos and videos by month, screenshots, videos, and large files.
  • Detect near-duplicate photos and blurry shots locally on your phone.
  • Let you swipe to keep, favorite, lock in the Private Vault, or stage unwanted files in the Review Bin.

All media scanning, duplicate/blur analysis, thumbnail rendering, and video playback run entirely on your device. We do not collect, upload, or store copies of your photos or videos on any external server.

3. User-Controlled Deletion

Swiping left moves items into a local Review Bin so you can double-check before removing anything. Files are only permanently deleted when you tap Delete in the Review Bin and confirm Android's system deletion prompt.

4. Favorites & Private Protected Vault

  • Favorites: Swiping up marks items in the in-app Favorites filter and syncs favorite status locally with your Android Gallery (Android 11+).
  • Private Vault Storage: Swiping down copies selected media into Phone Cleaner's private app-internal storage directory on your device, keeping those files hidden from public gallery apps.
  • Password & Biometric Protection: Your Vault Password/PIN is stored locally as a salted SHA-256 hash. Face and Fingerprint unlock use Android's native BiometricPrompt API; Phone Cleaner never accesses, reads, or stores your biometric data. Screenshot blocking and automatic locking protect your Vault when unlocked.

5. Local Preferences & Data Retention

Reviewed media IDs, Private Vault metadata, total space freed statistics, theme selection, and swipe settings are stored privately on your device using Android's local storage (Room database and DataStore).

Uninstalling Phone Cleaner permanently removes all app-local data, including files stored inside the Private Vault. Please restore any files you want to keep back to your gallery before uninstalling the app.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Phone Cleaner uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display adaptive banner ads and interstitial ads. While StructLabs does not directly collect personal information, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process device and usage signals—such as the Android Advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostic/crash data—to serve, measure, and personalize ads and prevent fraud.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

7. Children's Privacy

Phone Cleaner is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
